**## Projects**
  
### 1. Bank Loan Default Risk Analysis
An end-to-end analytics project focused on identifying patterns associated with loan defaults, performing risk segmentation, analyzing portfolio-wide default patterns, and developing practical risk-management recommendations.
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Power BI, DAX

### 2. MedTourEasy — Patient Conversion & Provider Performance Intelligence
A healthcare analytics project focused on analyzing patient conversion, treatment drop-offs, provider performance, patient satisfaction, and revenue to generate actionable business insights.
**Tools:** Power BI, DAX, Python, Pandas

### 3. Lead Lifecycle & Conversion Intelligence System
A business analytics project focused on analyzing closed-deal trends, lead types, business segments, and sales representative performance to identify meaningful business insights.
**Tools**: Python, Pandas, SQL, SQLite, Power BI, DAX.

**4. Customer Churn & Retention Analysis**
An end-to-end analytics project focused on analyzing customer churn patterns, identifying key churn drivers, and predicting customer churn risk using machine learning. Developed customer risk segments and actionable retention strategies, supported by interactive Power BI dashboards. 
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Power BI.
